# Analyzing Amateur Esports: Descriptive Statistics and EDA for Ld2L.GG

**Python · pandas · data visualization** · [View the code](https://github.com/smcgb/ld2l-data)

## The question

Professional esports get endless analysis. Amateur leagues get almost none. I wanted to find trends, patterns, and possible performance indicators in an amateur Dota 2 league, [Ld2L.GG](https://ld2l.gg).

## The approach

- Collected match data from the league
- Ran descriptive statistics and exploratory data analysis to find key statistics, outliers, and trends
- Looked at player performance, team composition, win rates, and related metrics
- Charted team KPIs over the season as rolling averages, and plotted the distribution of players by ranked tier to show the spread of skill
- Structured the final CSV so it can load straight into Power BI or Tableau, or feed machine learning models across seasons

## Who it's for

League organizers structuring fairer competitions, players looking to improve, and anyone curious about what the data says about amateur esports.
